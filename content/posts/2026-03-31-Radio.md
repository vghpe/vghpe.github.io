Radio La La is macOS app that lives in the menu bar and plays 3 radio stations. 

Goal: To get away from Spotify recommendations, expand my music exposure and make it easy to save songs I like. 
<!--more-->
Stations: 
- **FIP**: A commercial-free French "musical voyage" blending jazz, world, and indie genres.
- **KCRW** Eclectic24: The music-only stream from LA, offering 24/7 curated indie and global tracks without news.
- **KEXP**: A Seattle icon dedicated to independent music and high-energy live in-studio sessions.

**Features**:
- Sits inte Apple Menu bar
	- As an Icon (♫)
	- Artist + Track (Feist - Inside And Out)
	- Artist (Feist)
- See station played history
- Find song in Spotify (Opens Spotify)
- Copy track titles
- Can be controlled with Mac Media keys
	- Next/Previous - Cycle stations
	- Play - Stop / Reconnect
- Stations will show current playing even when paused
