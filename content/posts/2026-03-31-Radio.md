xxx is macOS app that plays 3 radio station. 

My need was to get away from Spotify recommendations. All 3 are playing hand curated music 24/7,  

**Stations:** 
- KCRW Eclectic 24 
- KEXP 
- France Inter Paris

**Features**:
- Sits inte Apple Menu bar
	- As an Icon (♫)
	- Artist + Track (Feist - Inside And Out)
	- Artist (Feist)
- See station played history
- Find song in Spotify (Opens Spotify)
- Copy track titles
- Can be controlled with Mac Media keys
	- Next/Previous - Cycle stations
	- Play - Stop / Reconnect
- Stations will show current playing even when paused
