Based on a game my brother designed during a road trip.

Play it here: [https://vghpe.github.io/sanoj-game/](https://vghpe.github.io/sanoj-game/)

My goal was to make something that's easy to share and play, like 2000s web-games where a link just led to a game you could play straight away. A game link today usually leads to an app to download, maybe it has sign-in, tutorials, etc. Play is 100+ clicks away.

It is possible to do high accessible web-games today. But it's really time-consuming handling both mobile and desktop input and resolutions. Making Sanoj responsive took as much time as designing the game itself.
